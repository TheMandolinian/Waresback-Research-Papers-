# HashHelix Whitepaper V1.9.5

**PDF:** [hashhelix-whitepaper-v1.9.5.pdf](hashhelix-whitepaper-v1.9.5.pdf)  
**Status:** Current corrected public framing  
**Author:** James Bradley Waresback / The Mandolinian

HashHelix Whitepaper V1.9.5 is the current implementation-grounded framing of HashHelix as a deterministic event sequencing and verification framework. It corrects earlier “time-aware” and “temporal” language by making the central claim more precise: HashHelix does not prove literal time; it provides deterministic sequence authority through accepted event ordering, lane progression, canonical payload binding, receipt/readback discipline, replayable projection, and future commitment structures.

The paper also defines the HelixWorks bridge posture: “UI proposes. Rust disposes. HashHelix commits. HelixWorks projects.” Whitepaper 2.0 is reserved for a later milestone after the full authority loop is proven through candidate submission, Rust/core validation and canonicalization, engine acceptance or rejection, receipt/readback, UI recording, and projection derivation.

For source posture and legacy notes, see [Source Checklist](../sources/source-checklist.md) and [Legacy HashHelix Materials](../legacy/README.md).
